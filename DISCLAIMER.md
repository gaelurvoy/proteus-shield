# Disclaimer

`proteus-shield-action` is a **measurement harness for defensive study only**.

- It measures, it does not protect. It does not harden, obfuscate, pack, or
  transform any software artifact.
- It does not implement, reproduce, or enable evasion of analysis, aggressive
  deception, or counter-attack against any analyzer, human or automated.
- Its "analyzer" for v0.1 is a documented, seeded scripted substitute — an
  explicit cost model, not an LLM, not a real reverse-engineering agent. Its
  token numbers are declared placeholders and must not be cited as real
  measurements against any actual agent.

Use this only to study the *measurement* of defensive cost on software you are
authorized to analyze, or on your own artifacts. Nothing here is legal, security,
or engineering advice for any specific deployment.
